Our story

From a yes on a bridge
to a day in the woods

We said yes on a spring afternoon by the water, with a bunch of roses and a very shaky pair of hands. Everything since has been planning for the day we get to do it properly — with all of you there.

We're putting together something warm and unfussy: long tables under the trees, candles everywhere, food worth staying late for, and the people we love in one place for once.

Juandré & Axel

The day

How it all unfolds

A rough shape of the day so you know when to arrive and when to kick your shoes off. Times may still shift a little.

  1. 14:00

    Bus leaves Norrköping

    Departing from Strand Hotell. If you're driving, head straight to Mem and aim to arrive by half past.

  2. 15:00

    The ceremony

    The short and important part. Please be seated by a quarter to.

  3. Afterwards

    Photographs & mingle

    We steal away for a few pictures while you find a drink and a spot by the canal.

  4. Early evening

    Dinner & speeches

    Long tables, candlelight, and a few words from those brave enough.

  5. Later

    Cake & first dance

    Sugar, then the bit we've been quietly practising.

  6. Late

    Dancing, then the bus home

    Until the music stops. The bus runs back to Norrköping afterwards — no one gets stranded.

Questions

Asked and answered

Can I bring a plus one?

Your invitation will say exactly who we've saved a seat for. We'd love to invite everyone's everyone, but the room only holds so many.

Are children welcome?

Yes — little ones are part of the family. Let us know ages on your RSVP so we can sort food and somewhere quiet for a nap.

I have a dietary requirement.

Tell us when you RSVP. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten‑free, allergies — all easily handled if we know in advance.

How do I get there, and where do I park?

Kanalmagasinet is at Mem Kanalmagasinet 1, just outside Söderköping — roughly half an hour from Norrköping by road. There's parking on site, and a bus from Strand Hotell in Norrköping at 14:00 that brings everyone home again afterwards.

Can I post photos?

Please do — after the ceremony. During it, we'd love to see faces rather than phones.

Who do I ask about anything else?

Just message either of us. We promise we don't mind the questions.
